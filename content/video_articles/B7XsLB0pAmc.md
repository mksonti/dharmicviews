```

+------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------+

| (EB-2 / EB-3)                      | Resulting in a 100-year+ waiting backlog     |

| Unlawful Border Crossings          | ~10,000 per DAY under Biden-Harris           |

### 1. The 100-Year Backlog Injustice
While high-skilled Indian professionals with Master's and PhD degrees wait over **100 to 150 years** for employment-based green cards due to a rigid 7% per-country cap, the Biden-Harris administration permitted over **10,000 illegal entrants per day** across the southern border [448, 449, 458].

### 2. The Mechanics of H1-B Minimum Wage Reform
Mohan Sonti clarified the controversy surrounding Trump's proposal to raise H1-B minimum wage floors [454, 455]:

```

                       | 1990 H1-B BASELINE     |

                                   |
                                   | 35 Years of Inflation
                                   v
                       +------------------------+
                       | CURRENT EXPLOITATION   |

                       +------------------------+

* **The $60,000 Inflation Trap**: When the H1-B minimum wage was codified in 1990, $60,000 represented a high salary [454]. By 2024, $60,000 became cheap labor, allowing outsourcing "body shops" to flood the lottery system with over 800,000 low-wage applications [454, 455].
* **Restoring Meritocracy**: Raising the H1-B minimum wage floor to **$120,000 - $150,000** eliminates low-cost outsourcing abuse and guarantees that genuine, highly compensated Indian STEM talent secures visa allocations [455].
