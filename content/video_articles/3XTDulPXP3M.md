## Generational Mathematics: Compounding Demographics

Mainstream commentators frequently assume that a small difference in fertility rates (e.g., 2.0 vs 3.5) produces negligible long-term changes [177, 180]. Generational compound mathematics proves otherwise [179, 180].

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               4-GENERATION (80-YEAR) POPULATION COMPOUNDING MODEL                       |
+-------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| Generation Step   | Group A: 100 Families (TFR = 2.0)| Group B: 20 Families (TFR = 4.0) |
+-------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| Gen 0 (Base)      | 100 Active Families (100%)       | 20 Active Families (100%)        |
| Gen 1 (20 Years)  | 100 Active Families (Replacement)| 40 Active Families (200%)        |
| Gen 2 (40 Years)  | 100 Active Families (Flat Line)  | 80 Active Families (400%)        |
| Gen 3 (60 Years)  | 100 Active Families (Flat Line)  | 160 Active Families (800%)       |
| Gen 4 (80 Years)  | 100 Active Families (Flat Line)  | 320 Active Families (1600%)      |
+-------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| Share Shift       | Drops from 83.3% down to 23.8%   | Surges from 16.7% to 76.2%       |
+-------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------+
```

### 1. The Generational Model Parameters
Mohan Sonti constructed a four-generation model spanning an 80-year human lifespan [179, 180]:
* **Generational Window**: 20 years, representing the mean child-bearing age for women (20 to 40 years) [179].
* **Group A (Replacement Rate)**: 100 families maintaining a Total Fertility Rate (TFR) of 2.0 children per woman [180]. Over 80 years, Group A remains flat at 100 active families per generation [180].
* **Group B (High Fertility Rate)**: 20 families maintaining a TFR of 4.0 children per woman [180]. Over 80 years, Group B doubles every generation: 20 -> 40 -> 80 -> 160 -> **320 families** [180].

### 2. The Tipping Point Result
In just 80 years, Group B expands from a 16.7% minority to a **76.2% absolute majority**, turning Group A into a minority without a single act of physical violence--purely through compounding mathematics [180, 184].
