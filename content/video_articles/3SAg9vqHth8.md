## Electoral Integrity: Statistical Anomalies and Reform Mandates

A critical dimension analyzed by Mohan Sonti is the vulnerability of the US election system and the 15-million-vote statistical anomaly between 2020 and 2024 [530, 535].

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               US PRESIDENTIAL ELECTION VOTE TOTALS (2012 - 2024)                  |

| 2012         | Obama (65.9M)     | Romney (60.9M)    | Baseline Standard          |
| 2016         | Clinton (65.8M)   | Trump (62.9M)     | Flat Line Baseline         |
| 2020         | Biden (81.2M)     | Trump (74.2M)     | +15M Unexplained Spike     |
| 2024         | Harris (71.0M)    | Trump (74.6M)     | Reversion to Baseline      |
+--------------+-------------------+-------------------+----------------------------+
```

### 1. The 15-Million-Vote Anomaly
* **Democratic Vote Trajectory**: Democratic presidential candidates received ~66 million votes in 2012 (Obama) and ~66 million in 2016 (Clinton) [535]. In 2020, Joe Biden's tally suddenly spiked to **81.2 million votes**--an unprecedented 15-million-vote jump achieved while campaigning primarily from his residence [535].
* **2024 Reversion**: In 2024, Kamala Harris's vote total dropped back to ~71 million, while Donald Trump maintained his consistent ~74.6 million base [535]. This 10-15 million vote disappearance reinforces concerns regarding mail-in ballot integrity in 2020 [535, 536].
