This spatial erasure is driven by two unaddressed existential factors: massive foreign-funded Christian missionary conversions in Vanvasi belts and a severe statistical anomaly pointing to "virtual voter migration" through multi-constituency voter registrations [23, 33, 34, 38].

### 3. The "Virtual Voter Migration" Anomaly
Because total state turnout remained flat at 66.53% and BJP's total vote percentage remained unchanged at 33%, the total collapse in seats across Divisions 1, 3, 4, and 5 can logically only be explained by **Virtual Voter Migration** [21, 22, 23, 30]:

* **Mechanism**: Organized networks execute duplicate voter registrations for individuals across multiple constituencies [23, 30, 41].
