# Did India Achieve the Goals of Operation Sindoor?

## Executive Summary & Core Premise

Following the Pahalgam terror attack targeting Hindu pilgrims, Bharat executed a swift, multi-dimensional military counter-offensive designated **Operation Sindoor** [135, 137, 701, 707]. In an analytical panel on The Jaipur Dialogues USA featuring Dr. Adityanjee and Mohan Krishna Sonti hosted by Vibhuti Jha, the military, strategic, and diplomatic outcomes of Operation Sindoor were evaluated against long-term national security benchmarks [135, 137, 702, 707].

The discussion demonstrates that Operation Sindoor achieved its primary strategic objectives: establishing a new military escalation baseline, destroying high-value terror infrastructure 100 kilometers inside Pakistan (Bahawalpur, Muridke), signaling a total end to "strategic restraint," and exposing Pakistan's complete economic inability to sustain conventional warfare [136, 702, 704, 733].

This 7-8 page document provides an exhaustive strategic critique, evaluating the military shift from covert strikes (Uri) to stand-off missile salvos (SCALP/HAMMER), multi-vector diplomatic signaling, the role of China and Turkey, and the inevitable internal implosion of Pakistan [136, 138, 707, 715, 734].
