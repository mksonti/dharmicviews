### 1. The Statutory Benchmark: 106th Amendment Act (2023)
The 106th Constitutional Amendment Bill (Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam) was introduced by the ruling BJP administration in 2023 [356]. It passed with unprecedented parliamentary consensus [356, 357]:
* **Rajya Sabha Yield**: Unanimous 100% approval across all present members [357].
* **Lok Sabha Yield**: Passed with near-total consensus, receiving only **two negative votes**, both cast by Asaduddin Owaisi's AIMIM [357].
* **Statutory Enactment**: Having received Presidential assent, the 106th Amendment is codified **Law of the Land** (Act of Parliament), guaranteeing 33% seat reservation for women in the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies [356, 357].

### 2. Procedural Delimitation and the 131st Amendment Obstruction
Under standard constitutional procedure, seat reservation takes effect following the first national census and subsequent boundary delimitation exercise [358]. To prevent delay beyond the 2029 general election (which would otherwise push implementation to 2034), the government introduced the 131st Amendment Bill [358, 359].

The 131st Amendment proposed expanding the Lok Sabha from 543 seats to **850 seats** based on 2011 census figures, allowing immediate allocation of 33% female seats without reducing existing male constituencies [358, 359]. However, the opposition INDIA block (led by Congress and supported by AIMIM) blocked the procedural vote, demanding manufactured sub-quotas to stall implementation [359, 360, 361].
